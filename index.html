How to Use Focus Mixer & Create Your Ideal Workspace

Focus Mixer combines a Pomodoro timer with 12 ambient sounds to help you focus. This guide walks you through the basics and useful techniques.

Getting Started

1

Blend Ambient Sounds

Click any sound in the central sound panel to start playback. A highlighted button indicates an active sound. Play multiple sounds at once to create your own ambient mix.

💡 Start with Rain or White Noise.
2

Adjust the Volume

Adjust each sound independently with the slider below its sound button. When layering sounds, balance the levels for a natural, calming atmosphere.

💡 A 70% primary sound and 30% supporting sound is a good starting balance.
3

Start the Timer

Press Start on the timer panel to begin a 25-minute countdown. Use the settings icon (⚙️) to adjust the focus duration. When the timer reaches zero, a notification sound plays and the timer switches to break mode.

4

Take a Break

After a focus session, the timer switches to break mode. Ambient sounds continue during your break, or you can choose a different sound set for a change of pace.

💡 Gentle sounds such as Birds or Bell work well during breaks.
5

Save a Preset

Save your favorite sound combinations as presets. Press Save to store the current settings in your browser, then recall them with one click next time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sound stops during playback

Browser power-saving settings may pause audio after a tab stays in the background for a while. Bring the tab back to the foreground and start playback again.

Can I use Focus Mixer on a smartphone?

Yes. On mobile devices, audio may stop when the screen turns off. Set the screen timeout to never, or tap the screen occasionally to keep it awake.

Can I use it offline?

Focus Mixer is a browser-based web app. Once the sound files have been cached after your first visit, the core features can work offline.

About Focus Mixer

Focus Mixer is a minimalist Pomodoro timer and ambient sound mixer built to support deep, sustained focus. Its distraction-free controls help you shape a workspace that suits the task at hand.

The Science of the Pomodoro Technique

Developed in the 1980s, the Pomodoro Technique alternates focused work intervals with short breaks. A clear time boundary can make it easier to get started, while regular breaks help manage fatigue during longer work sessions.

Why use focused intervals?

  • Sustained concentration: A defined work interval gives your attention a clear goal.
  • Regular recovery: Short breaks create space to reset between sessions.
  • Visible progress: Counting completed sessions helps you notice your working rhythm.

How Ambient Sound Helps

Ambient sound can make sudden noises and nearby conversations less noticeable by providing a steady background. Focus Mixer lets you layer sounds and adjust each volume to find a comfortable balance.

What is sound masking?
Sound masking uses background sound to reduce the contrast between a distraction and its surroundings, making that distraction less prominent to the listener.

Privacy & Technology

Timer settings, volume levels, and presets are stored in your browser and are not sent to Focus Mixer servers. Audio is delivered with the Web Audio API, so there is nothing to install. Read the Privacy Policy for details about cookies, analytics, and advertising.
